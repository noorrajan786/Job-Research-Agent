# Interview Prep Brief: Digital Assets Product Team Analyst (Global Payments), Bank of America

*Sourcing note: My research found no primary BofA press release for the 2026 items below, so treat them as reported rather than bank-confirmed. In the interview, say "I read that..." rather than stating them as fact.*

## Company snapshot
- Regulated national bank with deep wealth, trading, payments and custody relationships. Q3 results cited consumer banking, wealth management, commercial lending and markets.
- **Q3 (reported Oct 14, 2026):** Net income was $6.9B ($0.91/share), including a $1.2B regulatory settlement charge tied to anti-money-laundering matters. Revenue rose about 3% to $30B.
- **Digital assets so far:**
  - In July 2025, CEO Brian Moynihan said BofA was preparing to enter the stablecoin market and would act "when the time is right."
  - A third-party explainer (about three weeks old) says BofA has not issued a stablecoin or public deposit token.
  - Media report BofA in a multi-bank stablecoin consortium and a planned Clearing House tokenized deposit network (details below).

## What the role needs
- **Experience:** 3-5 years in financial services, banking, payments, fintech, product management, consulting or a related field.
- **Core skills:** Analysis and synthesis, influence without direct authority, comfort with ambiguity, project management, written and verbal communication (including executive presentations), attention to detail, and proficiency in Excel, PowerPoint and other standard business tools.
- **Nice-to-have:** Digital assets, blockchain, stablecoins, tokenization or emerging payments.
- **Day to day:**
  - Market, competitive and industry research, translated into actionable insights.
  - Strategy materials, business cases and executive communications.
  - Product requirements, roadmap tracking and documentation.
  - Risk and governance support (assessments, approvals, action items).
  - Coordination across Product, Technology, Operations, Risk, Legal, Compliance and Finance.
  - Commercialization and launch support.
  - Ad hoc projects for senior leadership and the Digital Assets team.

## Recent news to mention
- **Stablecoin consortium (articles ~early Sept 2026; exact date not found):** BofA is among participants alongside Goldman, Citi, Fidelity and Wells Fargo. The group said it would create a new company in 2H 2026 to support issuance, with a dollar stablecoin targeted for 1H 2027.
- **Tokenized deposit network (June 2026):** CoinDesk, citing the WSJ, reports that JPMorgan, Citi and BofA plan a shared network by mid-2027, operated by The Clearing House. Deposits would become blockchain tokens that move around the clock while staying inside the regulated banking system.
- **Leadership (reported ~July 2026, secondary sources):** Sonali Theisen was given an added mandate to design, build and govern the global digital assets platform. Adam Dixon stays on in digital asset transformation, covering tokenized deposits, stablecoins, digital collateral transfers, crypto settlement and custody.
- **Cross-border real-time payments (announced June 4, 2026):** Planned via Swift or CashPro, connecting to SPEI (Mexico), UK Faster Payments and India's UPI. It was expected to launch the following quarter. I haven't confirmed whether it has launched.
- **AskGPS Intelligence Hub (Sept 28, 2026):** Tools to help Global Payments Solutions employees interpret complex treasury information.
- **Regulation:** The GENIUS Act's operative date is January 2027.
- **Wealth and research (2025):** Forbes (Dec 2025) reported BofA recommends clients allocate up to 4% of portfolios to crypto. BofA research has called tokenization "mutual fund 3.0" and noted growing investor focus on tokenizing real-world assets.
